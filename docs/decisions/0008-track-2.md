# 0008. HackCure Track 2 (Web & Mobile) and how we frame the product

Status: accepted (2026-10-10)

## Context

The HackCure specification ([`../HackCure_Cahier_des_Charges.pdf`](../HackCure_Cahier_des_Charges.pdf)) defines three tracks.

- **Track 1, IoT** requires functional physiological sensors.
- **Track 3, AI** expects predictive models with bias control.
- **Track 2, Web & Mobile** covers care coordination, telemedicine and patient engagement, with synchronous and asynchronous care pathways and **strict tolerance of degraded networks in rural areas**. Its priority is **zero latency on the critical triage action**.

## Decision

We compete in **Track 2**. Borj-PiCare is a care-coordination platform for hospital wards.

- **Setting:** public hospital wards, **including regional and district hospitals** (hôpitaux régionaux / de circonscription), where internet is unreliable and staff are scarcest.
- **Coordination (core):** who to see first (NEWS2), who is free, call routing, escalation, assignment suggestions, the medication timeline.
- **Telemedicine:** a **remote on-call doctor** receives escalations on their phone with the NEWS2 trend and its explanation. They acknowledge or leave a short instruction. This works synchronously when online and asynchronously (queued, SMS fallback) on a poor connection.
- **Patient engagement:** the bedside call node with delivery feedback, and the Family Care Link.
- **Degraded networks:** the ward runs entirely on the local network with no internet. Gateway, nodes and apps store and forward. The demo includes a live "cut the network" moment.
- **Zero latency on triage:** the triage path (reading → NEWS2 → alert → phone → ack) never leaves the ward network. Its latency is measured and shown on the board, with a budget of under 1 second from button press to phone.

## Consequences

- The Raspberry Pi node and any sensors are **supporting hardware** that prove the resilience story. The pitch leads with the apps.
- What-if, assignment and the 3D twin stay **off the critical path**.
- Equipment maintenance stays out of scope (off-topic for the track).
